# Code of Conduct

Biro Soka is a game that people play at school, at home and with friends, and some of the people who play it and
help build it are young. We want everyone to feel welcome here.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume people mean well, especially newcomers.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Disagree politely and be open to being wrong.
- Keep it friendly for all ages: no harassment, hate, sexual content, or personal attacks, in code, issues, comments,
  pull requests, pen and desk art, or the funny lines the game shows.
- Respect privacy. Do not share other people's personal information.
- Do not spam, and do not post anything dangerous or illegal.

## What is not okay

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, trolling, and repeatedly ignoring a request to stop.

## Who this covers

Everyone taking part in the project: the GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any place where
people speak for the project.

## What happens when it goes wrong

Maintainers can edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and can warn, temporarily
block or permanently ban someone. We aim to be fair and to explain what happened.

## Reporting a problem

Tell a maintainer privately: use the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), or open a private report through the
GitHub repository's "Report content" option. Reports are kept confidential.

## Where this comes from

This is a short version in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which you can read for more detail.
